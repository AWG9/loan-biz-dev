Tips for this Template
Initial Outreach
Best sent Tue–Thu, 9–11am. Mention their previous loan amount to show you know them. Keep the subject line short and curiosity-driven.
Follow-Up 1
Wait 3–5 days after initial. Reference your last email briefly. Add urgency without pressure — mention limited availability.
Follow-Up 2 (Final)
Last touch. Keep it short and gracious. Leave the door open — many deals come from "last chance" emails.
Proposal
Fill in the loan amount and monthly payment before sending. Confirm numbers verbally first if possible. Clear next steps = faster close.
